# PathSignal — Evaluation worksheet

Do not tune on the trials you later report as an independent evaluation.

## Freeze before scoring

- Model and build identifier
- Score threshold and corridor definition
- Phone, browser or Expo version
- Fixed phone height, pitch and orientation
- Minimum useful warning distance, selected and marked before scoring
- Obstacle dimensions, positions and floor conditions
- Lighting and approach procedure
- Method for recording audible alert onset

Run stationary image checks first. Physical approach trials require a sighted, supervised operator able to stop before the object. Do not create an exposed hole or other hazardous test setup.

## Score consistently

- **Early** — the first audible alert occurs before the preselected warning-distance mark.
- **Late** — an audible alert occurs at or after the mark.
- **Missed** — no audible alert occurs by the end of a completed object-present trial.
- **False alert** — an obstacle alert occurs during an empty-path trial.
- **Incomplete** — camera failure, interruption or a procedure error prevents a valid trial. Report these separately.

The website's exported timestamps record completion and speech request, not audible onset or physical distance. Use an observer or synchronized recording for audible latency. Session logs alone cannot score early versus late.

## Report

Report the number of completed object-present and empty-path trials, early and late alerts, misses, false alerts and incomplete trials. If reporting proportions, include their denominators. Report measured warning distances only where recorded. Include failures and their conditions. Do not merge saved-image checks, tuning clips, synthetic examples and physical trials into one accuracy number.

## Accessibility checks

Use VoiceOver on the actual iPhone to check focus order, control names, status announcements and access to Stop and Repeat. Verify text scaling, portrait layout, contrast, camera-denial messaging and sound with the intended volume settings. Automated desktop checks cannot replace this review.
