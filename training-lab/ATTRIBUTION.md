# Data and model provenance

- Local videos: supplied by the PathSignal project owner for development. Original attachments are unchanged. Source hashes and the corrected direction labels are retained in `stair-direction/2026-11-23` manifests. The new local reference boxes were visually annotated by the assistant and are approximate, with no independent second review.
- Public pothole data: Farzad, *Pothole_Segmentation_YOLOv8*, version 1, 20 October 2023. Origin: https://universe.roboflow.com/farzad/pothole_segmentation_yolov8/dataset/1 . The originating project and exported data.yaml identify **CC BY 4.0**, confirmed on the project page on 23 November 2026. Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The Kaggle mirror https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/farzadnekouei/pothole-image-segmentation-dataset lists Apache 2.0; that discrepancy is preserved in the research metadata rather than silently replacing the originating attribution.
- Changes to the public data: segmentation polygons converted to enclosing boxes; publisher validation images divided deterministically into model-selection and evaluation subsets; one validation image with a near dHash match to training excluded. Publisher training augmentations retained. No claim of original collection or original neural-network architecture.
- Pothole candidate: Ultralytics YOLOv8n COCO pretrained weights, with transfer learning on the public pothole boxes. Ultralytics source/model licensing is AGPL-3.0 unless separately licensed: https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics . The application has an existing AGPL source offer. No model from this experiment has been published.
- Direction candidate: frozen torchvision MobileNetV3-small ImageNet1K-V1 features, with a newly trained linear direction head. Source and weight details: https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/stable/models/generated/torchvision.models.mobilenet_v3_small.html . Torchvision source: https://github.com/pytorch/vision . Do not describe ImageNet pretraining as PathSignal's own training.
- Existing comparison models: locally downloaded Roboflow `obstacle-detection-n3eec/1` (generic stairs) and `staircase-detection-mrpt2/1` (Upstair/Downstair). Their original artifact folders retain class order, metadata and checksums. Comparing these downloaded models does not change their redistribution status.

All new training and inference in this lab run locally. No user photos or videos are uploaded by the scripts. Package and publisher weight downloads require network access. No paid compute is used.
